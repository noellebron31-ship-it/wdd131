Website Planning Document

WDD 131 - Personal Trading Project Blueprint

Site Name

ApexTrader Insights (or BullishHorizon)

Reason for selection: The name implies a high-tier, strategic approach to navigating the financial markets. It positions the platform as a reliable resource for analyzing market trends, tracking assets, and sharpening execution skills.

Optional Domain Availability: apextrader-insights.net / bullishhorizon.io

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to act as a centralized hub for retail traders looking to improve their market technical analysis and strategies. The platform will provide real-time or curated mock market layouts, an interactive glossary of technical indicators (like Moving Averages and RSI), educational strategy walkthroughs, and a dynamic position-sizing calculator to help users manage financial risk effectively.

Target Audience Scenarios

The content of this website is structured to directly answer critical questions from potential users:

  • Scenario 1: "I am a beginner trader. How do I properly calculate my risk-to-reward ratio before opening a position so I don't lose too much capital?"
  • Scenario 2: "What are the core differences between day trading price action strategies and long-term swing trading indicators?"

Color Schema

This planning document dynamically reflects a modern, dark-mode financial layout palette:

Primary (Slate Dark)
Secondary (Bullish Green)
Accent (Bearish Red/Orange)
  • Primary Color (#1e293b): A sleek, deep slate charcoal used for major container backgrounds and structural headers to give it a premium terminal look.
  • Secondary Color (#10b981): A vibrant emerald green representing profit/growth, used for primary highlights, active navigation tabs, and success elements.
  • Accent Color (#f97316): A warning orange/red shade used selectively for risk alerts, call-to-action buttons, or calculator reset options.
  • Background/Text: Built with high contrast utilizing a crisp off-white for reading text over deep, clean backgrounds.

Typography

  • Heading Font: 'Inter', sans-serif — A modern, geometric typeface that mirrors clean UI layouts seen on professional trading dashboards.
  • Body Font: 'Roboto Mono', monospace — A clean, highly legible font ideal for presenting numbers, technical formulas, and asset metrics.
